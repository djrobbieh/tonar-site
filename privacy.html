Tonar Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

The short version

Tonar collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. Your music library, playlists, playback history and settings are stored only on your device.

Your media servers

When you add a source (SMB/NAS, WebDAV, S3, Plex, Jellyfin, Subsonic, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive), Tonar connects directly from your device to that server to browse and stream your files. Credentials are stored securely on your device and are only sent to the server you configured. We never see them.

Online lookups (optional, off-switchable)

Some optional features look things up online. When you enable one, Tonar sends only a track’s title and artist (and sometimes album and duration) to the relevant service — never your library as a whole, and never your identity:

  • Metadata & artwork — MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, Apple’s iTunes Search API
  • Lyrics — LRCLIB
  • Song info / About — MusicBrainz and Genius
  • Scrobbling — ListenBrainz (sends the tracks you play to your own ListenBrainz account, using a token you provide)

These requests contain no personal identifiers. Settings → Privacy has a master “Allow Online Lookups” switch that turns every one of them off at once, plus an individual switch for each service. You can also restrict all network use to Wi-Fi. With online lookups off, nothing about your listening leaves your device.

Third parties

Tonar contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. When you use the optional features above, the receiving service handles your request under its own privacy policy: MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive and ListenBrainz (metabrainz.org), Genius (genius.com), LRCLIB (lrclib.net) and Apple (apple.com). Casting to Chromecast and playing via CarPlay happen locally between your devices. Purchases and the free trial are handled by Apple through the App Store; Tonar receives only whether you’re entitled to the paid features — no payment details.
