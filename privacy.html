Tonar Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

The short version

Tonar collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. Your music library, playlists, playback history and settings are stored only on your device.

Your media servers

When you add a source (SMB/NAS, WebDAV, S3, Plex, Jellyfin, Subsonic, Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive), Tonar connects directly from your device to that server to browse and stream your files. Credentials are stored securely on your device and are only sent to the server you configured. We never see them.

Online metadata (optional)

If you enable online metadata or lyrics, Tonar queries public services — MusicBrainz, Cover Art Archive, the iTunes Search API and LRCLIB — using only track information (title, artist, album, duration). These requests contain no personal identifiers. You can turn this off, or restrict all network use to Wi-Fi, in Settings.

Third parties

Tonar contains no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs. Casting to Chromecast or playing via CarPlay happens locally between your devices.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.
