Tonar.

Hi-Res FLAC music player for iPhone and iPad. Stream your own library from NAS (SMB), WebDAV, S3, Plex, Jellyfin, Subsonic, Dropbox, Google Drive and OneDrive — with gapless playback, crossfade, offline downloads, an EQ, synced lyrics, song info, CarPlay and Chromecast.

Frequently asked

Why don't I see my music? Add a source under Sources and make sure your device can reach the server (for home NAS access on the go, a VPN such as WireGuard works well).

What counts as Hi-Res? Tracks with 24-bit depth or a sample rate of 88.2 kHz and above, in line with industry definitions.

Does Tonar use mobile data? Only if you allow it — enable “Wi-Fi Only” in Settings to block streaming and downloads on cellular.
